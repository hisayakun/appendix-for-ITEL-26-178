# Detailed Scoring Criteria for Question 3 (Section 6.2.1)

Detailed scoring criteria for items (1) and (2) in Question 3 of Table 5 of the paper. The criteria for 5, 2, and 1 points are given in Table 5; the criteria for 4 and 3 points are detailed below.

## Item (1)

- **4 points**: The proposed function exhibits high practicality but is relatively lacking in originality. It is within the range that many people would expect but remains a good idea that could significantly improve the convenience of the system.
- **3 points**: Practicality is recognized, but the idea is general. A basic function that many people would initially consider.

## Item (2)

- **4 points**: Uses appropriate programming terminologies and can explain the outline of implementation. However, the rationale for selecting data structures and specificity in detailed processing are relatively lacking.
- **3 points**: Basic terminologies, such as loop and conditional branching, are used, but the explanation is abstract or fragmentary. The overall picture of the operation of the program is vague.
